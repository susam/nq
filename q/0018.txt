A <em>tree well</em> is a depression that forms around the base of a
tree when snow accumulates around it.  The lower branches of trees
prevent snow from settling closely around the trunk, leaving a loose,
hidden cavity.

Tree wells can pose a danger to skiers and snowboarders, who may fall
into the concealed cavity and become trapped in the loose snow.
