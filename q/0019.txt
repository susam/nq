The Messier objects are a set of 110 astronomical objects catalogued
by the French astronomer Charles Messier.  M31 is the Messier
designation of the Andromeda Galaxy, the nearest major galaxy to the
Milky Way and visible to the naked eye under dark skies.  The
Andromeda Galaxy is also known as NGC 224 in the New General
Catalogue.

By contrast, M13 is the Great Globular Cluster in the constellation
Hercules, a dense spherical cluster of hundreds of thousands of
ancient stars.  M42 is the Orion Nebula in the constellation Orion, a
bright diffuse nebula and active stellar nursery.  M45 is the Pleiades
in the constellation Taurus, an cluster often called the Seven
Sisters.
