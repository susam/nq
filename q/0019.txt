The <em>Messier catalogue</em> is a list of 110 astronomical objects
originally compiled by the French astronomer Charles Messier.  The
first version was published in 1774 with 45 objects.  More objects
were added to the catalogue later by various astronomers thereby
expanding the list to 110 objects.

The Andromeda Galaxy is listed as M31 in this catalogue.  It is the
closest major galaxy to the Milky Way.  It can be seen with the naked
eye on a dark, clear night.  The Andromeda Galaxy is also known as NGC
224 in the <em>New General Catalogue</em>, compiled by the Danish
astronomer John Louis Emil Dreyer and published in 1888.

M13 is the Great Globular Cluster in the constellation Hercules.  It
contains hundreds of thousands of ancient stars packed closely
together.  M42 is the Orion Nebula.  M45 is the Pleiades, a star
cluster in the constellation Taurus, also known as the Seven Sisters.
