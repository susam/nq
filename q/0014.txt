A <em>dozen</em> is a group of twelve items.  A <em>dozen dozen</em>
therefore equals 144.  A dozen dozen is also known as a
<em>gross</em>.

A <em>score</em> refers to a group of twenty items.  A baker's dozen
refers to thirteen items.  This term originated from the practice
among bakers including a thirteenth piece of bread when selling a
dozen to err on the side of caution to ensure that minimum weight
requirements were being met.

The terms <em>quire</em> and <em>ream</em> refer to quantities of
paper.  A quire is typically 25 sheets of paper.  In an older system,
a quire referred to 24 sheets.  A ream is 20 quires.
