The brain of a domestic cat is roughly the size of a walnut.  It is
about 5 cm in length.  It typically has a mass between 25 g and 30 g.
A cat's brain usually has a mass of less than 1% of its total body
mass.  By comparison, the human brain has a mass of about 2% of the
total body mass.

Despite its small size, a cat's brain is highly specialised for
sensory information processing, balance and motor control.  Cats rely
heavily on vision, hearing, smell and whisker-based touch.  The feline
brain is good at observational learning and problem-solving as well as
retaining long-term memories.
