The flags of Indonesia and Monaco look quite similar.  Both have a
red-over-white bicolour design, but their proportions differ.
Indonesia's flag has a ratio of 2:3, while Monaco's flag has a ratio
of 4:5.  The shade of red differs slightly too.  Monaco's flag has a
slightly darker shade of red.

Neither flag includes a coat of arms or any other emblem.
Incidentally, the flag of Singapore has a red-over-white design too
but it also has a white crescent moon facing a pentagon of five small
white stars.
