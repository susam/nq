grammage of \( 80 \, \mathrm{gsm} \)?

A standard sheet of A0 paper has an area of \( 1 \, \mathrm{m}^2 \).
If we cut a sheet of A0 paper in half parallel to its shorter side,
each half is a sheet of A1 paper.  Thus a sheet of A1 paper has an
area \( 1/2 \, \mathrm{m}^2 \).  We can keep cutting the halves like
this to obtain sheets of A2 paper, A3 paper and so on.  Therefore the
area of a sheet of A2 paper is \( 1/4 \, \mathrm{m}^2 \) and that of a
sheet of A3 paper is \( 1/8 \, \mathrm{m}^2 \).  Similarly, the area
of a sheet of A4 paper is \( 1/16 \, \mathrm{m}^2 \).

The grammage \( 80 \, \mathrm{gsm} \) means that the paper has an area
density of \( 80 \, \mathrm{g}/\mathrm{m}^2 \).  Therefore the mass of
a sheet of A4 paper is

\[
  80 \, \mathrm{g}/\mathrm{m}^2 \cdot \frac{1}{16} \, \mathrm{m}^2
  = 5 \, \mathrm{g}.
\]
