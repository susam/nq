---

The term 'lat' is short for <em>latissimus dorsi</em>.  It refers to a
pair of large, flat muscles on either side of the back.  The <em>lat
pull-down</em> is a seated strength training exercise designed to
build the latissimus dorsi muscles.  It involves pulling down a
horizontal bar attached to weights.  The latissimus dorsi muscles are
also known as 'lats' among gym-goers.

The <em>lateral deltoid</em> is one of the three heads of the deltoid
muscle.  It is located on the outer side of the shoulder and is
responsible for lifting the arm away from the body.  Exercises such as
lateral raises are designed to develop this muscle.

The <em>trapezius</em> is a large, trapezium-shaped muscle that
extends from the base of the skull down to the middle of the back.

The options <em>lateral stabilisation</em> and <em>latent
strength</em> are merely distractors.  They can have a variety of
meanings depending on the context.  They are not related to the lat
pull-down exercise.
