ID3v2.3 Frame IDs
Computing, Audio
Susam Pal

---

According to the ID3v2.3 specification, which frame IDs identify the
song title, lead artist and year, respectively?

---

<code>TIT2</code>, <code>TPE1</code>, <code>TYER</code>

---

<code>TT2</code>, <code>TP1</code>, <code>TYE</code>

---

<code>TIT2</code>, <code>TPE1</code>, <code>TDRC</code>

---

<code>TIT1</code>, <code>TPE3</code>, <code>TCOP</code>

---

In an MP3 file, an ID3 tag stores metadata such as the title, artist,
album, year and copyright information.  ID3v2 tags organise this
information into frames.  Each frame has an ID that indicates what
information it stores.

In ID3v2.3, the <code>TIT2</code> frame stores the song title,
<code>TPE1</code> stores the lead artist or performer and
<code>TYER</code> stores the year.

In ID3v2.2, <code>TT2</code>, <code>TP1</code> and <code>TYE</code>
identified the same fields as <code>TIT2</code>, <code>TPE1</code> and
<code>TYER</code>, respectively.  ID3v2.2 used three-character frame
IDs.

ID3v2.4 uses <code>TDRC</code> for recording time, but this frame ID
is not defined in ID3v2.3.

The frame IDs <code>TIT1</code>, <code>TPE3</code> and
<code>TCOP</code> are valid in ID3v2.3, but identify a content group
description, a conductor and a copyright message, respectively.
