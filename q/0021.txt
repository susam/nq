Hart's Rules is the oldest continuously updated style guide in
English.  The first edition appeared in 1893 under the title <em>Rules
for Compositors and Readers at the University Press, Oxford</em>.  It
was compiled by Horace Hart, an English printer and biographer.

Although it was originally produced for internal use at Oxford
University Press, it soon gained a wide readership among authors and
printers.  Copies circulated informally and were even resold in London
bookshops without Hart's permission.  In response, Hart issued a
commercial edition in 1904 for a broader audience.  The handbook has
since been revised and expanded several times.  The most recent
edition, published in 2014, is titled <em>New Hart's Rules: The Oxford
Style Guide</em>, 2nd ed.
