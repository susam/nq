Hart's Rules is the oldest continuously updated style guide in
English.  The first edition appeared in 1893 under the title <em>Rules
for Compositors and Readers at the University Press, Oxford</em>.  It
was compiled by Horace Hart, an English printer and biographer.

Hart originally intended these rules for use within Oxford University
Press.  However, copies of the handbook soon found their way into the
hands of other printers and authors.  Some London booksellers were
even selling copies without Hart's permission.  This prompted Hart to
publish a commercial edition in 1904.  The handbook has undergone
several revisions and additions since then.

The most recent edition, published in 2014, is titled <em>New Hart's
Rules: The Oxford Style Guide</em>, 2nd ed.
