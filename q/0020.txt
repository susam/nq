---

What does the term <em>misophonia</em> mean in clinical psychology?

---

A strong emotional reaction to specific sounds

---

Fear of loud noises

---

Loss of hearing in one ear

---

An inability to recognise familiar voices

---

Heightened sensitivity to all sound

---

The word <em>misophonia</em> comes from the Greek words
<em>misos</em>, meaning hatred, and <em>phone</em>, meaning sound.
Literally, it means hatred of sound.

A person with misophonia may find certain everyday sounds very
disturbing.  For example, the sound of someone chewing, tapping their
fingers or breathing may cause irritation, anger or distress.
