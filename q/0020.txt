What does the term <em>misophonia</em> mean in clinical psychology?

---

A strong emotional reaction to specific sounds

---

Fear of loud noises

---

Loss of hearing in one ear

---

An inability to recognise familiar voices

---

Heightened sensitivity to all sound

---

The word <em>misophonia</em> comes from the Greek <em>misos</em>,
meaning hatred, and <em>phone</em>, meaning sound.  Literally, it
means hatred of sound.  In clinical psychology, however, this term
refers to a condition in which particular ordinary sounds such as
chewing, tapping or breathing trigger intense feelings of irritation,
anger or distress.  Unlike general sound sensitivity, the reaction is
usually tied to specific trigger sounds.
