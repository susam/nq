How many simple, undirected graphs can be formed with \( n \)
vertices?

---

\( 2^{n(n - 1)/2} \)

---

\( 2^{n(n - 1)} \)

---

\( 2^{n^2} \)

---

\( n!   \)

---

\( (n!)!  \)

---

A <em>simple graph</em> is a graph without loops or multiple edges,
i.e. no vertex has an edge that connects the vertex to itself and any
pair of vertices has at most one edge between them.

In an <em>undirected graph</em>, edges do not have a direction.  Each
edge is represented by an unordered pair of vertices.  On the other
hand, a <em>directed graph</em> has edges with direction.  Each edge
of a directed graph is represented by an ordered pair of vertices.

In a simple, undirected graph with \( n \) vertices, there are
\[
  \binom{n}{2} = \frac{n(n - 1)}{2}
\]
pairs of vertices.  For each pair of vertices, there may or may not be
an edge between them.  Therefore the total number of distinct simple,
undirected graphs that can be formed on \( n \) labelled vertices is
\[
  2^{n(n - 1)/2}.
\]
