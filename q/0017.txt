The word <em>Arctic</em> comes from the Greek word <em>arktikos</em>,
which means <em>of the bear</em> or <em>near the bear</em>.  This, in
turn, comes from <em>arktos</em>, the Greek word for <em>bear</em>.

The name refers to the constellation Ursa Major, the Great Bear, which
is prominent in the northern sky.  The Arctic Circle is the region of
the Earth located under this northern sky, near the constellation of
the bear.

By contrast, <em>Antarctic</em> comes from the Greek
<em>antarktikos</em>, meaning <em>opposite the bear</em>.
