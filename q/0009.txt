According to the ECMA-147 standard, a 3&frac12; inch floppy disk case
has an external height of 94.0 mm (&pm;0.3 mm) and an external width
of 90.0 mm (&plus;0.4 mm/&minus;0.1 mm).  This means that the case
must be at least 93.7 mm tall and 89.9 mm wide.  In inches, these
minimums are approximately 3.69 and 3.54 inches.

A case that is exactly 3.5 inches tall and 3.5 inches wide would not
conform to the standard.  The term '3&frac12; inch' is only a nominal
size.  It does not represent the actual height or width of the case.
