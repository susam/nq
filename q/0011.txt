<em>The Mother of All Demos </em> was a live computer demonstration
presented on 09 Dec 1968 at the Fall Joint Computer Conference in San
Francisco.  During the 90-minute presentation, the audience was shown
a working computer mouse, windowed graphical interfaces, hypertext,
real-time text editing, video conferencing and collaborative work on
networked computers.  Such technologies were unusual in 1968, but many
of them eventually became part of everyday computing.

The demonstration was given by Douglas Engelbart, a computer scientist
at the Stanford Research Institute.  Engelbart and his team had built
the computer system used in the demonstration.
